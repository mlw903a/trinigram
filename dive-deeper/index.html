Frequently Asked Questions

About Trinigram

What is Trinigram?

Trinigram is a simple assessment tool that reveals your current balance across three dimensions of faith: Glory (connecting with God the Father), Pursuit (following Jesus' example), and Attunement (experiencing the Spirit's guidance). In just a 5-minute quiz, you'll discover where you're strong and where you might grow to cultivate a more balanced Christian life.

Who created Trinigram?

Trinigram was created by Mark Williams, a former tech CFO whose analytical mindset and deep faith led him to develop this unique approach to spiritual assessment. Along with his wife Toni (an author at TCHaas.com), Mark is active at Riverbend Church in Austin and leads nonprofit initiatives focused on environmental stewardship and children's welfare.

Why did you create this?

We created Trinigram because we saw how many Christians struggle to find balance in their spiritual lives. As someone who spent years in the analytical world of finance, I (Mark) wanted to bring data-driven clarity to spiritual growth while honoring the profound mystery of the Trinity. Our goal is to help believers understand and strengthen their connection with all three Persons of the Trinity in practical, measurable ways.

Trinigram is entirely self-funded and free from commercial interests. If you'd like to support our mission, we welcome you to explore our nonprofit initiatives at Oceanbags.com, where we provide ecological backpacks to kids in crisis, and KidsAndPlanet.com, where we create and publish books that inspire a heart of giving in children.

Why focus on balance?

Most of us naturally drift toward imbalance in our faith, favoring one Person of the Trinity over others. This can lead to distortions in our spiritual life—like a three-legged stool with uneven legs. True flourishing comes from balanced engagement with all three Persons of the Trinity. When Glory, Pursuit, and Attunement work together in harmony, your faith experience becomes exponentially more profound.

The Two Foundations

What is Trinitarian Design?

The Trinity is an infinite mystery that has inspired centuries of theological debate. Rather than entering those deep waters, Trinigram focuses on one practical dimension: Design. We believe God's design is perfect—warts and all. Everything in creation has its place, function, and rhythm, including you with all your flaws and struggles.

In our framework:

  • The Father is the Designer who crafted all creation with intention
  • The Son is the Example who shows us what perfect humanity looks like
  • The Spirit is the Guide who helps us live out that perfect design

What is Trinitarian Balance?

Think of your faith journey not as separate lanes, but as a vibrant, interconnected ecosystem. Glory, Pursuit, and Attunement aren't isolated practices—they're deeply interwoven, each dimension enriching the others. When you deepen your awareness of God's design (Glory), it shapes how you follow Christ's example (Pursuit). As you tune into the Spirit's guidance (Attunement), it illuminates your understanding of both.

This isn't about achieving perfect balance, but about embracing ongoing, graceful movement—expanding in some areas, gently releasing in others, always with compassion for yourself and others.

Understanding Your Results

What do my Glory, Pursuit, and Attunement scores mean?

Glory (Father):
Measures your capacity to recognize and appreciate God's design in creation, others, and yourself. High Glory = regular experiences of wonder, awe, and gratitude. Low Glory might indicate judgment, criticism, or indifference blocking your view of God's brilliance.
Pursuit (Jesus):
Reflects how intentionally you study and apply Jesus' approach to life. High Pursuit = actively learning from and emulating His manner and wisdom. Low Pursuit might suggest you're not consistently connecting Jesus' example to daily choices.
Attunement (Spirit):
Gauges your awareness of and openness to the Holy Spirit's presence. High Attunement = regularly sensing the Spirit's nudges and finding peace in His guidance. Low Attunement might indicate distraction or self-reliance drowning out the Spirit's voice.

Should I focus on my weakest dimension?

Yes! Start with your lowest percentage. Small shifts in your weakest area will have the biggest impact because the dimensions multiply rather than just add to your faith. When you raise your lowest dimension, it lifts your whole experience of faith—like strengthening the shortest leg of a stool.

Growing Your Balance

How can I grow in Glory?

Glory is your expansion playground. Imagine a busy executive who sees everything as a problem to solve, or a frazzled parent who's lost the wonder of childhood. Growing in Glory means rediscovering awe in the everyday—whether it's noticing the intricate design of your morning coffee's steam patterns or recognizing the divine image in that difficult coworker.

Embrace:Wonder, Awe, Appreciation, Stewardship, Compassion, Gratitude
Release:Thanklessness, Cynicism, Indifference, Criticism

How can I grow in Pursuit?

Pursuit is your learning studio. Think of the longtime Christian who's stuck in autopilot, or the new believer overwhelmed by "What Would Jesus Do?" scenarios. Growing in Pursuit is about making Jesus' example real in your context—whether that's applying His patience to your commute or His revolutionary love to workplace conflicts.

Cultivate:Study, Contemplation, Wisdom, Humility, Gentleness
Move Past:Rigidity, Passivity, Inconsistency, Superficial Engagement

How can I grow in Attunement?

Attunement is your spiritual listening room. Picture the person who only looks for God in dramatic moments, or the analytical type who dismisses intuition entirely. Growing in Attunement means developing sensitivity to the Spirit's steady presence—recognizing that nudge to reach out to someone or that peaceful "no" when considering a decision.

Amplify:Awareness, Listening, Peace, Discernment, Trust
Soften:Ego-centricity, Distraction, Busyness, Self-reliance

About This Approach

Is Trinigram for everyone?

Trinigram isn't for everyone, and that's okay. If you're not a Christian, you might not connect with our Trinitarian foundation. For some, our emphasis on perfect design might feel off if they prefer focusing on human brokenness. We're not here to compete with your existing faith practices, but to offer a complementary lens that celebrates God's design as a path to growth.

How is this different from other assessments?

Rather than focusing on personality types or spiritual gifts, Trinigram specifically examines your relationship with each Person of the Trinity. We don't offer rules or fix what's not broken—we help you see yourself as a masterpiece that might just need some realignment. Our goal is integration, not perfection.

Why don't you address theological controversies?

While scholars continue their important work on Trinitarian theology, we've chosen to focus on a single practical dimension that can transform daily faith experience. We believe this focused approach makes the profound truth of the Trinity more accessible and actionable for everyday Christians.

What Next?

I've taken the quiz. Now what?

  1. Start with your weakest dimension
  2. Practice small, daily actions to strengthen it
  3. Remember that growth isn't linear—it's a dance
  4. Be patient with yourself as you cultivate balance
  5. Return regularly to reassess your journey
